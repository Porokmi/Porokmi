# Porokmi Sambahamphe

M.A. Economics student at Fordham University with a background in finance and accounting. I work on applied econometrics in Python and on tools that make financial reporting and reconciliation faster.

### Projects
- **[Urban–rural gaps in household living conditions in Nepal](https://github.com/Porokmi/nepal-urban-rural-gaps-replication)**. Uses the 2022 Nepal Demographic and Health Survey to examine gaps in clean cooking and housing and estimate whom different targeting rules would reach.
- **[FDI and business investment in Canada](https://github.com/Porokmi/canada-fdi-investment)**: does foreign direct investment crowd in business investment? Working paper, code, and data for the 1981–2018 baseline and the extension through 2024, with cointegration and error-correction models in Python.
- **[Bank Rec Workbench](https://github.com/Porokmi/bank-reconciliation-engine)** · [live demo](https://porokmi.github.io/bank-reconciliation-engine/): a browser-based bank reconciliation tool I built for my own work. It cut my reconciliation time roughly in half.

Interests: applied economics, monetary and development policy, and financial data analysis.

I build with AI tools (Claude, Codex) and explain in each project how they were used.
